# Controls update v37

Touch steering uses a floating origin on the chosen screen half with a 4.5% radial dead zone. Full throw is 12% of the short viewport edge, bounded to 36–64 CSS pixels. Response is 0.5*t + 0.5*t², with a 1.45 touch gain. Movement is immediate; visual bank smoothing is independent. Mouse and keyboard keep their existing movement speeds, with diagonals normalized. Camera follow response increased from 7 to 18 per second.

Evade moves 15 world units in 0.30 seconds, bounded by the flight corridor, with a shared 1.8-second activation-to-activation cooldown. Direction locks on activation. Neutral directional taps and fully blocked outward taps do not consume a charge. There is no invulnerability. Normal steering does not add speed during an evade. A complete axis-angle tumble is visual only; collision and ring contacts use a stable translated flight body. Simulation limits forward/lateral travel during evades to 0.3 units per collision sample. Existing ordinary-flight collision sampling is retained.

Controls Setup is accessible from home, the header, and sound/settings. Steering side and directional/split button layout are saved separately under stargaze-controls-v1; skyward-v1 progress is unchanged. Default: left steering, directional evade on the right. Touch IDs are independent; pointer cancellation, lost capture, pause, app blur, restart and world changes clear steering. Pausing freezes an active maneuver and cooldown; resuming continues it. Restart resets both. E offers a keyboard directional evade.

Validation: tests/controls-runtime.mjs exercises touch response, eight directions, neutral/boundary behavior, direction lock, cooldown, pause, mirrored touch layouts, pointer isolation/cancel, mouse speed, thin-obstacle contact, stable collision body, restart, preference persistence and progress isolation, all 15 world/difficulty combinations, all 20 ships and frame independence. Existing runtime, fairness, mechanics, modes, rewards and fleet checks supplement this.

Supported control-browser is unavailable in this managed environment. No animated browser review, actual multi-touch browser injection, audio listening, or physical phone/performance test was performed. Phone-sized dimensions in the runtime adapter verify logic only, not rendered layout or feel. Check thumb reach, progressive sensitivity, direction readability and roll comfort on a physical phone.
