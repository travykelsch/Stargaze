# Canyon planet artwork

Asset: `canyon-planet-v19.png`. Created with the built-in image generation tool for Skyward Flight's Easy world. The image is used as a distant, transparent matte painting, blended into daylight haze; it is not a gameplay screenshot.

Prompt: A single beautiful distant terrestrial alien planet, isolated on a transparent background. Complete centered circular planet, jade and moss-green organic continents, turquoise and blue ocean basins, detailed ivory cloud systems and natural fractal terrain. Soft sunlight from the upper right, gently shaded left limb, delicate pale cyan atmospheric rim. Painterly photoreal fantasy matte painting suitable for a huge planet looming in a bright alien sky. Avoid gas-giant stripes, sine waves, marbling, rings, stars, text and interfaces.
